## Provider security boundary

Gmail/Slack ingestion and extraction remain disabled pending explicit decisions about approved scopes and consent, permitted workspaces/mailboxes/channels, and content protection, retention, deletion, and disconnect behavior. Provider credentials must be configured through managed secrets, never chat or source control. Until then, continue provider-independent work with synthetic fixtures; do not call provider APIs or persist message bodies.
